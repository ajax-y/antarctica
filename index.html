Already Bought? Safe Disposal

Multi-layer plastic cannot be recycled curbside. Avoid dumping in regular municipal bins:

RECOMMENDED STEPS:

1. Rinse and dry wrapper thoroughly.
2. Stuff into a clean PET bottle to make an Eco-Brick for green construction, or hand over to Dry Waste Collection Centers (DWCC).
